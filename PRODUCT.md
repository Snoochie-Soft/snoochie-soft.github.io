# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing static site on GitHub Pages (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step), repo Snoochie-Soft/snoochie-soft.github.io, served at https://snoochie-soft.github.io/. Stays static.

## Users

The site serves four audiences, all confirmed by the owner:
- People from Google Play (or the open web) deciding whether to install one of the apps.
- Google and AdMob reviewers: the site is the developer's required web presence (privacy policies, terms, app-ads.txt, contact must stay reachable).
- Partners and press judging the studio.
- Search visitors arriving on app names.

## Product Purpose

Snoochie Soft is a tiny independent studio publishing Android apps worldwide through Google Play. The site is the studio's storefront: it shows each app credibly (real screens), links to Play, and hosts the legal pages. Success is installs from the site, a clean developer-verification trail, and each app ranking in search on its own page.

## Positioning

Small apps made properly: no account, works offline, honest pricing, no shaming. Only a studio that ships exactly these apps can claim this exact set of promises.

## Operating Context

- Six live Android apps, each gets its own sub-page (and per-language versions): Szybciej Się Nie Da (English in 50 days, no grammar, pl), Mossy (cosy mini-games for adults with ADHD, en), Fake Chat Maker & Text Message (en), Loopclass (school planner, 47 languages), Offline Audio Transcriber (6 site languages already), Christian Pray (pl.snoochiesoft.christianpray, in production on Play, no site page yet).
- More apps will be added over time, so the home layout must scale beyond six.
- Existing site language is Polish with English accents; the new primary language is English.
- Apps are translated for the whole world on Play, so the site must follow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static hosting only. Existing files that must keep working: app-ads.txt, google7bf347b91b7e4bc3.html (Google verification, must never disappear), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, per-app privacy and terms pages, Play badges in img/badges.
- Site languages (9): English (primary), Polish, German, Spanish, Japanese, plus four more picked from the most popular Google Play languages. Never Russian (owner rule, no exceptions).
- Each app has its own sub-page. Real screenshots exist: Loopclass 8 (fastlane, light and dark), Fake Chat Maker 6, Transcriber 4 in 6 languages, Szybciej and Mossy already on the site.
- Open: Christian Pray copy and screenshots still to be gathered.

## Brand Commitments

Name Snoochie Soft. Existing studio mark (S in a square) and per-app icons are fixed assets. Tenets already on the site and binding: no account, offline, honest.

## Evidence on Hand

Real app icons, store screenshots, Play badges, demo videos (Transcriber foreground-service clips). No testimonials, download counts, or ratings may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show every app with its real screens, never stock imagery.
2. English first, then the world: structure for many languages and many apps.
3. Legal and verification pages are first-class and always reachable.
4. Small studio, high craft: every app card earns its place by showing the product working.
5. Static, fast, no tracking surprises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect light and dark preference (already on the site), keyboard and screen-reader access, readable CJK typography for the Japanese version.
