Open Mossy and you're in a little night forest. Tap a plant to play. Not in the mood to choose? Today's Mix picks three games for you each day.

  3. Play for 1 to 3 minutes. The game adapts quietly, so it stays just challenging enough.
  4. Your garden grows. Then you close the app and get on with your day.

Questions

Is Mossy a medical or therapy app?

No. Mossy is a set of games for focus and relaxation. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or cure any condition.

Do I need an ADHD diagnosis to play?

Not at all. Mossy is made with ADHD brains in mind, and it's for anyone whose thoughts like to wander.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Every game works offline. You only need a connection if you decide to buy Full Unlock through Google Play.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and play. There's no sign-up, no email and no login.

What does Full Unlock include?

All 16 games, for one payment of $6.99 (or the local equivalent on Google Play). It isn't a subscription and nothing renews. Before you buy, you can try each of the 10 locked games once for free.

Will I lose progress if I skip a few days?

No. There are no streaks to lose. Moss doesn't die when you forget it. It just waits.

How do I delete my data?

Everything lives on your phone. In the app, open Settings, then Data, then Reset progress. Or clear the app's storage in Android settings, or simply uninstall. Details are in the privacy policy.
