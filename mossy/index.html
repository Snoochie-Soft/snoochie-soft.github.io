What it does

  • 16 tiny games, 1 to 3 minutes a round.
  • Learn any game in about 10 seconds.
  • Games adapt quietly, so they stay just challenging enough.
  • Today's Mix: three games picked for you each day.
  • Music generated as you play, so it never loops the same ten notes.
  • A little forest garden grows as you play.

How it behaves

  • 6 games free, forever. Try each locked game once, free.
  • Full Unlock: all 16 games for one payment, no subscription.
  • Works offline. No ads, no account, no tracking.
  • No streaks to lose and no shame screens.
  • Reduced motion, colour-blind mode and adjustable haptics.

Moss doesn't die when you forget it. It just waits. Show up when you can; your garden will still be there. Your scores, badges and garden are saved only on your phone. The one outside party is Google Play, which handles the Full Unlock purchase.

Mossy is a set of games for focus and relaxation. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose, treat or cure any condition.
