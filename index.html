House rules

Three promises we press into our releases. Each app page lists the ones it keeps, in its own words.

  1. No account

    Open the app and use it. No sign-up wall and nothing to log in to before you can try it.

  2. Works offline

    Notes, games and recordings stay on your phone and keep working on a plane, on a train or with the Wi-Fi off.

  3. Honest pricing

    What an app costs is shown before you pay. No surprise walls, no guilt trips, no shaming.
