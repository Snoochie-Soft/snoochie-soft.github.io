Pug Bubble Dig - privacy policy

Snoochie Soft does not create user accounts and does not itself collect personal data. Your progress, settings and unlocked finds are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the game.

The game shows a Google AdMob banner ad. For this, Google and its partners may process the device advertising ID, IP address, device data and ad interactions and, with your consent (a consent form appears on first launch in the EU and UK), personalise ads. Details: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You can change or withdraw consent in your device settings (Google > Ads) and reset your advertising ID. The game has no payments or other third-party analytics.
