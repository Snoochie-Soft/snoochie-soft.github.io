Loopclass: Schedule Planner

Android app by Snoochie Soft

Loopclass keeps your week in one place: the timetable, homework and exam deadlines, and your grades with averages. It shows what is on right now, what is next and what is due, and reminds you before it matters.

  • Today and week views with a live "now" class and countdown
  • Tasks and exams with reminders, priorities and early warnings
  • Grades per subject, weighted averages, percent, letters or 1–6 scale
  • Light and dark themes, accent colours, English and Polish
  • Optional Google sign-in to back up your data to your own Google Drive
  • Free with a small ad banner; a one-time Premium purchase removes it
