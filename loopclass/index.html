LoopclassSchool timetable, homework and grades

Three screens from the app

Side A: what it does

  1. A1A weekly timetable with rooms, teachers and A/B weeks
  2. A2Homework and exams with several reminders each
  3. A3Grades with weights, averages and GPA: percent, letters A–F or the Polish 1–6 scale
  4. A4Home screen widgets: your next class with a countdown, and your open tasks
  5. A5Today's ring closes when every task due today is done

Side B: how it behaves

  1. B1No account and no internet needed
  2. B2Optional backup to your own private Google Drive folder
  3. B3No server of ours: your planner stays on your device
  4. B4Every planner feature is free, with one small banner ad
  5. B5A one-time Premium purchase removes the ad. No subscription

Read the privacy policy

Liner notes

Overdue tasks get a calm label, never a red counter. Every effect has its own switch, plus Minimum and Maximum presets.

Reminders are scheduled again after a restart, and class times stay put when you change time zones.

Small print

Made for high school and college students (13+). Ads are served by Google AdMob.
